# Future Ideas & Product Concept Backlog

These represent long-term product concepts and feature ideas to be specified spec-first in the future.

---

## 1. AI Memory Layer
- **Goal**: Local-first vector embeddings of past notes and wishes using Gemini or WebGPU-based transformers to remember shared connection histories and automatically recommend deep wish topics.

## 2. Interactive Relationship Circles
- **Goal**: A dynamic visual representation mapping your connections into emotional proximity circles (e.g. *Inner Circle*, *Outer Circle*, *Professional*), helping users prioritize spontaneous check-in intervals.

## 3. Gift Memory & Purchase Tracking
- **Goal**: Integrate lightweight purchase link suggestions based on interests, tracking gifted histories over the years to avoid repeat items.
